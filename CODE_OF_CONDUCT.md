# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for
everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or derogatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
